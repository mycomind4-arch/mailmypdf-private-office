# Estate & Legacy Document Organizer

The Estate & Legacy Document Organizer is a Gold Standard Private Office workflow for organizing an estate and legacy document collection into a structured, review-ready matter.

## AI responsibilities

- Classify and organize source documents.
- Extract factual details with source provenance.
- Build a chronological record from document and event dates.
- Surface possible gaps, duplicates, and inconsistencies for human review.
- Map people, assets, documents, and events at a factual level.
- Prepare professional-review questions and optional correspondence.

## Explicit limitations

AI observations are assistive. The workflow must not determine legal validity, beneficiary rights, tax treatment, investment recommendations, fiduciary duties, or the legal effect of a document. Important observations must remain traceable to source material and be reviewed before reliance.

## Privacy

The workflow should minimize sensitive data collection. Do not require passwords, authentication credentials, Social Security numbers, or full bank account numbers. Use masked identifiers when an account reference is necessary.

## Workflow lifecycle

The workflow uses the same Gold Standard stages as the other Private Office matters and retains explicit human approval before consequential correspondence or mailing.
